Delivery, refunds & privacy

Applies to orders placed on this website.

Delivery

  • We deliver within Lahore only. Home delivery is free.
  • The minimum order is Rs 299.
  • Online ordering is currently available for our DHA Phase 4 branch. For other branches, please call to order.
  • After you place an order, our team calls your mobile number to confirm it. Please keep your phone nearby — we may not be able to dispatch an order we can't confirm.
  • Delivery times depend on distance, traffic and how busy the kitchen is. Every karahi is cooked to order.

Payment

  • Cash on Delivery — pay the rider in cash when your order arrives (or at the counter for pickup).
  • JazzCash, Easypaisa or bank transfer — only pay into the account details we share with you when we confirm your order, and send us the transaction ID (TID) on WhatsApp quoting your order number.
  • Prices on the website are in Pakistani Rupees (Rs) and are our DHA menu prices.
  • We will never ask for your card PIN, OTP or wallet MPIN.

Changes, cancellations & refunds

  • To change or cancel an order, call 0347 4001410 with your order number before we start cooking. Once your food is on the flame it can't be changed or cancelled.
  • If something is wrong with your order — a missing item or a problem with a dish — call us as soon as possible, and we'll make it right.
  • If an advance payment needs to be refunded (for example, an order we can't fulfil), we refund it to the same wallet or bank account it came from.

Privacy

  • At checkout we ask for your name, mobile number, email and (for delivery) your address. We use them only to prepare, confirm and deliver your order and to contact you about it.
  • We don't sell or share your details with anyone, except as needed to deliver your order.
  • If you tick “Save my details”, they're stored only in your own browser on this device, so the form is pre-filled next time. Untick it on your next order to remove them.
  • Your cart and chosen branch are also remembered in your browser.
  • To ask about or delete the details we hold for your orders, call or WhatsApp 0347 4001410.
