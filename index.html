SINCE 1947

Est. 1947 · Lakshmi Chowk

The Original, Since Partition.

Butt® Karahi has been serving Lahore since 1947, shortly after Partition, from its flagship at Lakshmi Chowk. Bone-in chicken karahi, cooked to order in oil, butter or desi ghee, with hand-made masala — no shortcuts, then or now.

Over the years, plenty of restaurants across Lahore have borrowed our name. The original is at Gita Bhavan Building, Lakshmi Chowk, McLeod Road — with official branches only at DHA Phase 4, Batapur, Shalimar and Shadbagh. If it isn't one of these, it isn't us.

Good to know

Questions

Where do you deliver, and is there a delivery charge?

We deliver across Lahore, and home delivery is free. The minimum order is Rs 299.

How do I pay?

Choose Cash on Delivery (or pay at the counter for pickup), JazzCash, Easypaisa or bank transfer at checkout. For wallet and bank payments, only pay into the account we give you when we confirm your order.

How do I know my order went through?

You get an order number (like BK-261002-1234) on screen as soon as you place it, and our team calls your mobile number to confirm. You can also send the order to us on WhatsApp in one tap.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Call us on 0347 4001410 with your order number before we start cooking. Every karahi is cooked to order, so it can't be changed once it's on the flame.

Which branches take online orders?

Online ordering is open at DHA Phase 4. For Lakshmi Chowk, Batapur, Shalimar and Shadbagh, please call to order — the prices on this site are our DHA menu prices.

Is the Butt Karahi near me the original?

Our only locations are Lakshmi Chowk (the original, since 1947), DHA Phase 4, Batapur, Shalimar and Shadbagh. Anywhere else using our name isn't us.
